# Power BI Dashboard

## Bank Marketing Campaign Analytics Dashboard

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of bank marketing campaign performance and term-deposit subscription behavior.

The dashboard was developed using the cleaned Bank Marketing dataset and focuses on customer characteristics, campaign performance, contact effectiveness, and economic indicators.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

Provides a high-level view of campaign performance.

Key metrics and analysis include:

- Total Contacts
- Successful Subscriptions
- Conversion Rate
- Previous Contacts
- Average Call Duration
- Monthly Conversion Trends
- Conversion by Contact Type
- Conversion by Age Group
- Conversion by Previous Campaign Outcome

### 2. Customer Analysis

Analyzes subscription behavior across customer characteristics.

Key areas include:

- Job Profile
- Age Groups
- Education
- Marital Status
- Housing Loan
- Campaign Contact Frequency
- Average Call Duration

### 3. Campaign Performance

Evaluates the effectiveness of campaign activities and contact strategies.

Key analysis includes:

- Conversion Rate by Campaign Contacts
- Conversion Rate by Day of Week
- Conversion Rate by Contact Method
- Conversion Rate by Previous Contacts
- Conversion Rate by Default Status

### 4. Economic Insights

Examines the relationship between campaign conversion and selected economic indicators.

Key indicators include:

- Euribor 3-Month Rate
- Employment Variation Rate
- Consumer Confidence Index
- Consumer Price Index
- Employment Index
- Monthly Conversion Trends

## Key Dashboard Insights

- Overall campaign conversion rate was **11.27%**.
- Previous campaign success showed the strongest observed conversion.
- Conversion generally declined with repeated campaign contacts.
- Cellular contact performed better than telephone contact.
- Conversion varied across customer age, education, and employment groups.
- Economic indicators showed substantial variation in observed conversion rates.
